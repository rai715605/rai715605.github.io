Blox Fruits - Guide

Leveling Tips

Use an Elemental Fruit or Buddha with a sword or melee fighting style to grind quickly.
The Human race is fine to use, but Ghoul is better (though harder to obtain).
Complete quests, get or buy XP boosts, and defeat bosses to get experience points quickly.

Leveling Strategies

Step 1 Do quests.
Turning in quests will net you a good amount of experience points. You'll usually get led through level-appropriate areas with quests. So you can break up the monotony of questing with grinding and vice versa!

Step 2 Grind enemies.
If you're bored with simply talking to NPCs and questing, you can mindlessly kill mobs to gain experience (this is, however, generally considered to be a pretty bad option as enemies give far less XP than quests).

Step 3 Get XP boosts.
Pay Robux to make leveling faster by buying XP boosts through the Roblox store. You can also search online for working codes that give you free XP boosts. A list of working codes can be found here

Step 4 Spend your skill points wisely.
Put 60% of your points to melee and 40% to defense. So every time you level up, put 2 in Melee and 1 in Defense. Once melee is maxed, you can put those skill points in Sword. This will make grinding faster and easier.
